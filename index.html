Our Story

A tiny roastery with a big heart.

Small Town Coffee Roastery started as a way to bring better coffee into quiet Ontario mornings. We roast in small batches, pay attention to the little details, and keep our operation intentionally small so we can stay close to the people drinking our coffee.

Most of our orders still come through Instagram messages, texts, and conversations at local spots. If you’re reading this, you’re probably one of those people — and we’re really glad you’re here.

What we care about
  • Fresh, balanced coffee that feels cozy, not snobby.
  • Roast profiles that highlight sweetness and comfort.
  • Keeping it local, small, and easy to order.
  • Helping you brew better at home, whatever setup you have.
